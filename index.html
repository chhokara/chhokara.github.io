My story

I’ve been through this search many times.

01

Where I started

I studied computer science at SFU, worked through co-ops, and stayed on for a master’s in big data. On paper I was ready. The job search still caught me off guard.

02

What the search was really like

Applications that went nowhere. Interview loops that ran five rounds. Behavioural questions I knew the answers to and still fumbled. Most of what I learned, I learned by getting it wrong first.

03

Why I do this

I’m now a software engineer. The most useful advice I got was specific and honest, and it was hard to find. I’d like to make it easier to find for the next person.
